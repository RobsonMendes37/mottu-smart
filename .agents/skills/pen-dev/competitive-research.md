# Competitive research

Use the [whiteboard guide](./whiteboard.md) to compare products side by side on the canvas.

Start with the supplied company or product. Understand who it serves and what problem it solves, then find a handful of relevant competitors. Include alternatives that solve the same problem differently when useful. Ask for clarification only when it would materially change the research.

## Build the board

- Keep the user's product and each competitor open in its own column. Explain why each is relevant.
- Pick one real task the customer needs to complete. Trace it across products using documentation or demos. Link the evidence at the step where the workflows diverge and explain what that difference costs or saves the customer.
- Compare what matters to this user, such as positioning, workflow, features, or pricing. Add a compact comparison table if it makes the differences easier to see.

Finish with the most useful takeaways and opportunities to investigate. Separate observed facts from interpretation; a feature you couldn't find isn't necessarily absent. Compare prices on the same basis and make unknowns visible.
