# Recruiting profiles

Use the [whiteboard guide](./whiteboard.md) to compare candidates' professional profiles.

Start with the supplied names, emails, resumes, or profile links. Use direct LinkedIn links first; otherwise search by name and professional context such as an employer or role. Check matches against the supplied history rather than choosing the first result. An email alone may not be enough to identify someone.

## Build the board

- Label each candidate and keep their profile open beside the evidence that identifies them. If several people fit, keep the plausible matches or a search browser and ask for a distinguishing detail. Attribute experience only once the identity is supported.
- Separately, for each supplied role requirement, link the strongest professional evidence for that candidate. For a material gap, give the specific question that would settle it, such as “Which part of the migration did you own?” Without requirements, keep the comparison neutral.

Account for everyone in the input, including people whose profiles couldn't be found or accessed. Distinguish missing evidence from a missing skill, and keep resume claims separate from profile observations when they disagree.

Use professional evidence, not sensitive traits or inferred personality. Keep private contact details off the board unless needed, and do not upload resumes to third-party services without permission. Let the user handle sign-in and leave outreach and hiring decisions to the recruiter.
